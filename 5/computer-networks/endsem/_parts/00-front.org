* Formula sheet
SUMMARY: Everything numeric on one screen. Rewrite this from memory the night before and the morning of.

** Delay
#+begin_example
  transmission delay   d_trans = L / R
                       L = packet bits, R = link rate in bits per second

  propagation delay    d_prop  = d / s
                       d = distance in metres, s = propagation speed

  nodal delay          d_nodal = d_proc + d_queue + d_trans + d_prop

  traffic intensity    La / R
                       below 1 the queue drains, at 1 it explodes

  end to end, N links  N x (L/R) + sum of d_prop + sum of d_proc
                       the L/R term repeats because every hop
                       receives the whole packet before forwarding it
#+end_example

** Throughput and utilisation
#+begin_example
  throughput           min(Rs, Rc, Rcore)

  bandwidth delay product   R x d_prop  bits that fit in the pipe

  stop and wait        U = (L/R) / (RTT + L/R)

  pipelined            U = (N x L/R) / (RTT + L/R)      capped at 1

  frames that fill a window   (R x RTT) / L   (round trip, not BDP)
#+end_example

** Windows and reliability
#+begin_example
  Go-Back-N     max window = 2^k - 1        k = sequence number bits
  Selective Repeat  max window = 2^(k-1) = half the sequence space
#+end_example

** Subnetting
#+begin_example
  block size        B = 2^(32 - prefix)
  usable hosts      H = 2^(32 - prefix) - 2
  mask in octet     the octet the mask cuts through, 256 - B is the step

  ladder   /30 = 2    /29 = 6    /28 = 14   /27 = 30
           /26 = 62   /25 = 126  /24 = 254

  rule     smallest prefix n where 2^(32-n) - 2 >= required hosts
#+end_example

** Link layer
#+begin_example
  minimum Ethernet frame   2 x t_prop x R    and at least 64 bytes
  10 Mbps slot time        512 bit times = 51.2 microseconds

  CSMA/CD efficiency       1 / (1 + 5 x (t_prop / t_trans))

  CRC check bits           equal to the degree of the generator
  fragment offset unit     8 bytes
#+end_example

** Congestion control
#+begin_example
  effective window    min(cwnd, rwnd)

  slow start          cwnd doubles every RTT until ssthresh
  congestion avoidance   cwnd grows by 1 MSS per RTT

  throughput          about  (0.75 x W) / RTT
                      or     (1.22 x MSS) / (RTT x sqrt(p))
#+end_example

* The twenty traps
SUMMARY: Each line is a mistake that has cost marks before. Read this last, ten minutes before you walk in.

1. Transmission delay is not propagation delay. Transmission is L/R and
   depends on the packet and the link rate. Propagation is d/s and depends
   only on distance. A short packet on a long fibre has a huge propagation
   delay and a tiny transmission delay.
2. Every hop pays the transmission delay again. A packet crossing three
   links pays L/R three times, not once.
3. Store and forward means the whole packet arrives before the first bit
   leaves. Do not overlap the L/R terms.
4. Queuing delay is the only delay that congestion changes. The other three
   are fixed by the packet, the link rate, and the geography.
5. Bandwidth is not throughput. Bandwidth is the capacity of one link.
   Throughput is what the path actually delivers, and it is the minimum
   across the path.
6. TCP numbers bytes, not segments. A segment with seq 90 and a payload of
   20 bytes makes the next segment's seq 110.
7. The ACK number is the next byte expected, not the last byte received.
8. SYN and FIN each consume one sequence number. Payload plus one for each
   of those flags.
9. In the reverse direction the roles swap. If A sent seq X with a payload
   of N bytes, B replies with ack X + N.
10. A checksum verification that does not end in all ones means the segment
    is corrupt. Do not round it off and call it close enough.
11. Forget the end-around carry on a one's-complement sum and the answer is
    wrong by one.
12. Go-Back-N retransmits the lost packet and everything after it. Selective
    Repeat retransmits only the lost one. If the question says GBN, do not
    describe SR.
13. A subnet has two reserved addresses. Usable hosts is 2^(32-n) minus 2,
    never 2^(32-n).
14. Every subnet address must be a multiple of its own block size. A /27
    cannot start at .96 if a /26 already occupies .64 to .127.
15. VLSM allocates largest first. Allocate smallest first and you will
    fragment the space and fail the alignment rule.
16. ARP is not used for a host on another subnet. The sender ARPs for its
    default gateway. This is the most commonly lost mark in the whole paper.
17. The fragment offset counts units of 8 bytes, not bytes.
18. The Ethernet frame minimum of 64 bytes excludes the preamble. The
    preamble is 8 bytes and is not counted.
19. A switch breaks collision domains but not broadcast domains. A VLAN or a
    router breaks broadcast domains.
20. Distance vector converges by gossip and bad news travels slowly. If a
    link fails, the cost climbs one hop at a time unless split horizon or
    poisoned reverse is in use.

* Exam technique
SUMMARY: How to spend 3 hours to get the most marks for what you know.

** How marks are actually given
A 5-mark answer needs five distinct points. A diagram counts as one or two.
A 10-mark answer needs a diagram plus six to eight points plus one worked
example.

Write the diagram first, even if you are unsure. Diagrams earn marks on
their own and they organise the prose that follows.

Name the thing. "The ARP request is broadcast at layer 2" earns a mark.
"The request goes out to everyone" does not.

** The order to answer in
Answer the numerical questions first. They are objective, they are fast, and
you cannot bluff them. Then the diagrams. Then the comparisons. Then the
essay questions.

Leave the last 15 minutes for checking arithmetic. A subnetting answer with
a wrong broadcast address loses marks that the method had already earned.

** The shape of a numerical answer
Always show three lines, even when you can do it in your head.

#+begin_example
  formula
  substitution with units
  answer with units and a box
#+end_example

A correct number with no working loses marks. A correct formula with one
arithmetic slip keeps most of them.

** When you do not know the answer
Write the definition you do know, then the diagram, then the comparison
table. Partial credit is generous on this paper. An empty page scores zero.

** Reading the question
The verb tells you the shape of the answer. Compare means a table.
Explain means numbered points. Discuss means points plus a judgement.
Design or deploy means a table with the columns named in the question. Draw
means a diagram with labels.

If the question lists the columns it wants, use exactly those columns, in
that order, with those names. Free marks.
