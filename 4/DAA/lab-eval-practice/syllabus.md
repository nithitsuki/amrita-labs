Here is the exact list of sorting algorithms you need to master for your lab evaluation, categorized by their underlying design techniques as outlined in your course and lab materials.

### 1. Incremental / Quadratic Sorts

These algorithms typically exhibit $O(n^2)$ worst-case time complexity but are foundational for understanding iterative comparison and in-place sorting:

* **Bubble Sort** 

* **Selection Sort** 

* **Insertion Sort** 

* You must also prepare to analyze and implement an **improved version of Insertion Sort** optimized for random data.

### 2. Divide and Conquer Sorts

These algorithms break the dataset into smaller subproblems and typically achieve an optimal $O(n \log n)$ average-case time complexity for comparison-based sorts:

* **Merge Sort** 

* **Quick Sort** 

### 3. Non-Comparison / Linear-Time Sorts

These algorithms exploit the mathematical properties of the input data to bypass the $\Omega(n \log n)$ lower bound of comparison sorts:

* **Counting Sort** 


* **Radix Sort** 


* **Bucket Sort** 



### 4. Advanced / Hybrid Sorts

These algorithms utilize advanced data structures or combine multiple sorting techniques to guarantee worst-case efficiency:

* **Heap Sort** 


* **Introsort (Introspective Sort)**
